Institutional Integration Framework (FAQ)

🔒 Does Droppo take custody of transactional clinical funds?

No. Droppo operates purely as a stateless, zero-custody mathematical orchestration framework. It computes multi-party allocation data matrices locally within your secure infrastructure and packages them into cryptographic, bank-ready instructions. Settlement execution occurs entirely on the Reserve Bank of India's sovereign Digital Rupee (e₹) core ledger layer via your partner bank's whitelisted CBDC nodes.

🌐 Is the ledger framework ABDM & ABHA compliant?

Yes. The transaction payloads generated by the `@droppo/core` engine map natively to the data formatting protocols enforced by the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM). The SDK cleanly isolates financial metadata from Protected Health Information (PHI), ensuring all automated multi-splits operate alongside existing Ayushman Bharat Health Account (ABHA) billing registries without data-privacy exposure.

⚡ What is the maximum processing latency for real-time reconciliation?

The localized `@droppo/core` deterministic multi-split math processes on the host invoicing server in under 2 milliseconds. Outbound cryptographic token signature handshakes sent to our secure Mumbai data nodes execute in under 15 seconds, facilitating instant, transaction-level auditing validation and eliminating manual end-of-month accounting loops.

🔧 How do we handle custom division percentages for multi-wing clinical units?

The engine configuration constructor natively accepts flexible, dynamic allocation arrays. Engineering teams can programmatically map custom splitting parameters (e.g., separating pharmacy inventories, surgical consulting fees, and base facility overhead weights) per invoicing department using standard JSON attributes, completely automating your multi-departmental payment ecosystem.
